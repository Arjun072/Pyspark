df= spark.read.format("csv") \
    .option("header",True) \
    .option("inferschema",True) \
    .option("delimiter",":") \
    .load("dbfs:/databricks-datasets/learning-spark-v2/people/")



x=df.withColumn("Category"
                ,when (col("salary")>=90000,"Executive")
                .when (col("salary")>=70000,"senior")
                .otherwise ("staff")
                  )


x.select("salary","firstname","category").show()

🔥 FULL OUTER JOIN — Real-Time Scenario
🏢 Scenario:

A company merged two systems:

Old HR system
New HR system

Business wants:

Show ALL employees and ALL departments
even if data mismatch exists.


Solution: 



emp_data = [
    (1, "Kamna", 101),
    (2, "Rahul", 102),
    (3, "Anjali", 103),
    (4, "Vikram", 104)
]

    (101, "HR"),

    (103, "Finance")
]


dept_df=spark.createDataFrame(dept_data,["dept_id", "dept_name"])

x= emp_df.join(dept_df,emp_df.dept_id==dept_df.dept_id,"full")
